Questions.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay for the project once. Your platform and hosting bills stay the same and keep going to the same companies.

Do you host my website?

No. The new design is built on the platform you already use, so nothing moves, and nothing depends on Arcane Designer afterward.

Which platforms do you work with?

Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and GoDaddy cover most sites. On something else? Send the link and you’ll get a straight answer.

Do I have to share my password?

No. Most platforms let you add a helper account with its own login. When the project ends, you delete it.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly feedback comes back. You’ll get a rough timeline with your quote.

How does payment work?

Half at the start and half at launch, through Stripe invoices. Card or bank transfer both work.

What if my site needs more than the starting price covers?

You’ll get a written quote before anything starts, and it won’t change without your OK.

Do you write the words too?

Your existing text gets tidied and tightened, and short new sections get written where a page needs them. Rewriting every page from scratch can be added to the quote.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for research, sketching ideas, building faster and catching mistakes. Every design and page is still reviewed by a person before you see it.

Where are you based?

Spokane, Washington. The work is done remotely, so it doesn’t matter where your business is.

Got an old website?

Send the link. You’ll get back a short list of what’s worth changing and what it would cost.
