What I look for

Most old websites have the same problems.

This is the kind of thing I notice in the first minute on a site. The business is made up, but every problem on it is real, and common.

my notes:
  1. The phone number is tiny. On a phone you’d have to pinch and zoom just to tap it, and for a plumber, that call is the whole point of the site.
  2. The coupons expired in 2013. Visitors notice, and they start wondering what else is out of date.
  3. Built for 2009 screens. A third of a modern monitor is empty, and on a phone everything shrinks to fit.
  4. This paragraph was written for search engines, not people. “Spokane plumber, Spokane Valley plumber” reassures nobody.
  5. “Best viewed in Internet Explorer 8.” Microsoft retired it years ago.

How it works

One project, start to finish. Then I’m out of your hair.

No retainer, no contract that renews itself. You get a better website and the keys to it.

  1. Send me your site

    I’ll look it over and tell you honestly whether a redesign is worth it.

  2. Answer a few questions

    Your customers, what you like, your hard no’s. About 20 minutes.

  3. Pick from three designs

    Clickable versions of your actual site, so you can try them like the real thing.

  4. I build it

    Right on the platform you already use. Your hosting and logins stay yours.

  5. A week of fixes

    Spot something after launch? I fix it. Then you remove my access.
