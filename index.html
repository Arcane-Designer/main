What usually needs fixing.

This plumber is made up. The problems are real, and they show up on a lot of sites.

northbankplumbing.com
12345
  1. 1The phone number is tiny. Hard to tap on a phone, and for a plumber, the call is the whole point.
  2. 2The coupons expired in 2013. People notice, and wonder what else is out of date.
  3. 3Built for old screens. Empty space everywhere on a big monitor, and everything shrinks on a phone.
  4. 4Written for search engines, not people. “Spokane plumber, Spokane Valley plumber” reassures nobody.
  5. 5“Best viewed in Internet Explorer 8.” That browser has been gone for years.
