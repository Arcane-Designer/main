Questions.

How do I start using AI?

Pick one free tool, like ChatGPT or Claude, and use it for something you actually need this week. Ask it to draft an email, plan a meal or explain a bill. Real tasks teach you faster than tutorials. Our free 7-day guide gives you one small task a day to build the habit.

Do I need to pay for AI tools?

Not to start. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Copilot and Perplexity all have free versions. Paid plans add more use and better models, and most people only upgrade once they’re using a tool every day.

What’s inside a kit?

Each kit is a download with copy-and-paste prompts, short screen-share videos of us doing the work and a first project to finish. You can start the day you buy it.

Can I buy a kit for my team?

Yes. A lot of business owners buy the AI for Marketing kit for their marketing person or team. For a whole team, the Complete Bundle covers everything.

Is an AI course or certificate worth it?

It depends on your goal. University certificates in AI marketing often cost $2,000 to $5,000 and take months. If you want to use AI in your work, hands-on practice gets you there faster and for far less. That’s the idea behind our kits.
