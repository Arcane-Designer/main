Questions

The fine print, in plain words.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay for the project and that’s it. Your hosting and platform bills (Squarespace, Wix, WordPress hosting and so on) stay exactly as they are, paid by you to them.

Do you host my website?

No, and that’s on purpose. I build on the platform you already use, so you never have to move your site or depend on me to keep it running.

Which platforms do you work with?

Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, GoDaddy and Showit cover most of what I see. If you’re on something else, send me the link and I’ll tell you whether it works.

Do I have to give you my password?

No. Almost every platform lets you add me as a collaborator with my own login. When the project is done, you remove me with one click, and I send you a short checklist to confirm nothing of mine is left behind.

How long does it take?

Most projects take two to three weeks from questionnaire to launch. The biggest factor is how quickly feedback comes back.

How does payment work?

Half when we start, half when your new site goes live. I send invoices through Stripe, and you can pay by card or bank transfer.

What if my site needs more than the starting price covers?

Then I tell you before anything starts. You get a written quote, and nothing changes from it without your OK.

Do you write the words on my site?

I tidy and tighten the words you already have, and I’ll write short new sections when a page needs them. If you want every page rewritten from scratch, we’ll include that in the quote.

Do you use AI?

Yes. I use AI tools to move faster, try more design ideas and catch mistakes. Every design decision and every page is checked by me before you see it.

Do you only work with Spokane businesses?

Spokane and the surrounding area is my focus, since I can meet you in person. I’m happy to work with businesses elsewhere too.

Send me your website. I’ll tell you what I’d change.

No pitch deck and no pressure. If your site is already in good shape, I’ll tell you that too.
