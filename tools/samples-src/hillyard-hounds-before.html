Welcome to our website and blog!

Hi everyone and welcome to the brand new Hillyard Hounds website! We are so excited to finally be online. Hillyard Hounds is a full service dog grooming salon and doggie daycare located in the Hillyard neighborhood of Spokane.

We offer baths, haircuts, nail trims, ear cleaning, teeth brushing and more. Our daycare is a safe fun place for your pup to play while you are at work! All dogs must be current on vaccinations (Rabies, DHPP, Bordetella). Please call to schedule a temperament test before your dog's first daycare visit.
