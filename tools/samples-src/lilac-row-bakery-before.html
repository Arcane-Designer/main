Welcome to our website!

Our famous Sourdough Loaf

Thank you for visiting the Lilac Row Bakery website! We are a small family owned bakery located in Spokane, Washington. We have been baking breads, pastries and cakes for our neighbors for many years and we are so happy you stopped by.

All of our breads are made from scratch every morning using only the finest ingredients. Our sourdough starter is over 10 years old! Please take a look around our site to learn more about what we offer. Don't forget to sign our Guestbook!!
