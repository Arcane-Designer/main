Your First 7 Days with AI

What the week looks like

  1. Day 1Pick one tool

    Set up a free AI account and get comfortable with the chat box.

  2. Day 2Ask for real help

    Hand it something on your actual to-do list.

  3. Day 3Tell it about you

    Give it context about your work, so its answers fit you.

  4. Day 4Write something

    Draft an email, a post or a bio with it, then make it yours.

  5. Day 5Plan your week

    Use it to sort your week and break a big task into steps.

  6. Day 6Try a second tool

    Run the same task in another tool and compare.

  7. Day 7Finish a project

    Take one small project from start to done with AI’s help.
