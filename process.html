From old site to new one, in six steps.

What happens, and what you’ll need to do along the way.

6 steps
01

Send your site

Use the contact form or email a link. You’ll get back what’s worth changing, and a price.

02

Deposit and questionnaire

Half the price up front, by card or bank transfer. Then a short questionnaire about your customers, your taste and what has to stay.

03

Three designs to choose from

Three different directions, built as real web pages you can click through on your phone. Pick one, or mix parts of each.

04

One round of changes

Send one list of changes. They get made, and you sign off before anything is built.

05

Build and launch

The new design goes onto the platform you already use. You add a helper login, so your password stays yours. The second half is due at launch.

06

A week of fine-tuning

For a week after launch, anything you want adjusted gets fine-tuned. Then you delete the helper login, and the site is entirely yours.

Yes, we use AI.

It’s how we keep projects quick and prices low without cutting corners on quality. We just didn’t want it to be a surprise.

Questions.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay for the project once. Your platform and hosting bills stay the same and keep going to the same companies.

Do you host my website?

No. The new design is built on the platform you already use, so nothing moves, and nothing depends on Arcane Designer afterward.

Which platforms do you work with?

Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and GoDaddy cover most sites. On something else? Send the link and you’ll get a straight answer.

Do I have to share my password?

No. Most platforms let you add a helper account with its own login. When the project ends, you delete it.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly feedback comes back. You’ll get a rough timeline with your quote.

How does payment work?

Half at the start and half at launch, through Stripe invoices. Card or bank transfer both work.
