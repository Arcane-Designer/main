Getting started

How do I start using AI?

Pick one free tool, like ChatGPT or Claude, and use it for something you actually need this week. Ask it to draft an email, plan a meal or explain a bill. Real tasks teach you faster than tutorials. Our free 7-day guide gives you one small task a day to build the habit.

What is a prompt?

A prompt is just what you type to the AI. Good prompts give context: who you are, what you want, who it’s for and what a good answer looks like. Think of it like briefing a smart new coworker on their first day.

How long does it take to learn AI?

You can do something useful in your first hour. Getting comfortable takes a couple of weeks of everyday use. Building automations takes longer, but it’s closer to a weekend project than a semester.

Do I need to pay for AI tools?

Not to start. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity all have free versions. Paid plans add more use and better models, and most people only upgrade once they’re using a tool every day.

Choosing tools

What’s the difference between ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity?

They overlap a lot. ChatGPT is the all-rounder most people try first. Claude is great for writing and long documents. Gemini fits right into Google’s apps. Copilot lives inside Microsoft Word, Excel and Outlook. Perplexity is built for research, with sources linked in its answers.

What’s the best AI for a small business?

The one your team will actually use every day. If you live in Google Workspace, try Gemini. If you live in Microsoft 365, try Copilot. For marketing writing, ChatGPT and Claude are both strong. Most small businesses end up using two.

Is it safe to put business information into AI?

Treat it like email. Don’t paste passwords, card numbers or private customer data. Check each tool’s settings for whether your chats are used for training, and use business plans when you’re handling sensitive work.

Marketing with AI

Can AI write my social media posts?

Yes, and it does a good job once it knows your brand. Give it examples of posts you like, describe your customer and tell it your voice. Then have it draft a week at a time and edit before you post.

How do I get AI to sound like my brand?

Write a short brand guide: who you serve, how you talk, words you use and words you never use. Paste a few of your best posts. Save that as a reusable instruction, and every draft starts closer to your voice.

What does automating marketing with AI mean?

It means the AI makes content on a schedule without you typing a prompt each time. For example, a recurring task that drafts next week’s posts every Friday. Our AI for Marketing kit walks you through setting one up.

Will AI replace marketers?

It’s changing the job. Marketers who use AI get through more work and spend more time on ideas and strategy. That’s why business owners are buying AI training for their marketing people now.

Learning AI

Is an AI course or certificate worth it?

It depends on your goal. University certificates in AI marketing often cost $2,000 to $5,000 and take months. If you want to use AI in your work, hands-on practice gets you there faster and for far less. That’s the idea behind our kits.

Can a beginner build an app or tool with AI?

Yes. Tools like Codex write the code from plain-English instructions. You won’t become a programmer overnight, but you can build a small, useful tool, like one that drafts and schedules your posts.
